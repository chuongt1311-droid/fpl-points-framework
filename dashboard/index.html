    Not a promotion signal.     Pre-registered decision rule — GW12 review

    Switches champion from M0 to a challenger only if ALL five conditions clear. Full text in docs/DECISION_RULE.md.
    1. Challenger's top-40 rank correlation beats M0's by a margin whose gameweek-clustered 95% CI excludes zero (real GW1-11 hindsight data, not this backtest).
    2. Challenger wins ≥8 of 12 individual gameweeks on points-per-week of the recommended XI.
    3. Challenger's calibration curve is no worse than M0's.
    4. Challenger's required sources reported healthy in ≥10 of 12 gameweeks.
    5. The mechanism by which the challenger wins can be stated in one sentence — an unexplained win gets investigated, not promoted.
